Abstract

The paper sets rules for changing knowledge, its grounds, norms and chosen representations, connecting them with history, publication and current admission. A finite revision model passed 73 checks with fixed inputs (0 failed); three model defects were found and fixed during preparation. In one observation of a file operation, a separate copy failed finalisation in a previous update (301 of 302 save operations succeeded) and the service gave no cause; the full archive still matched the canonical sources (348 of 348 hashes). No real distributed backend, authentication, books or classifier were used, and 36 subject and load setups remain planned and not executed. Superiority over simply replacing the last record is not shown.

Claims and their basis

  • Seven coordinates are distinguished: existence of content, assessment of its grounds, applicability, normative acceptance, package publication, choice of current state and completion of delivery. Success on one does not change another. Basis type: authors-domain-assessment; seven is not claimed necessary.
  • The finite machine passed 73 checks: 24 authored vectors, 39 negative controls, 5 finite enumerations and 5 positive checks. Basis type: finite-test. The count 73 and the 66 configurations use different units.
  • Three defects of the first model version were found by the authors and fixed: a boolean accepted as an integer receipt generation, a receipt confirmed without an observed publication effect, and the path . accepted as a manifest member. A second agent reviewed 12 boundary observations and found no new blocking defects; this is not expert review. Basis type: finite-test.
  • In the single R0015 observation, one separate copy stayed at an old version while the archive was intact; the cause was not disclosed. The audit shows 301 of 302 saves and 348 of 348 hashes. Basis type: finite-test; one operation, not repeated; the revision model (MM_A0364) was NOT read.
  • Dependency analysis distinguishes AND/OR structures of sufficient grounds. Basis type: authors-domain-assessment; completeness of dependency discovery was not measured.
  • The RU10 cross-check (kit authors, one assistant) is partly verified: the r1 report confirms two rejected packaging attempts, a first-adapter defect (empty Blueprint list) and an unrebuilt knowledge package. Retention of R0 history is not verified from the open reports. Basis type: authors-domain-assessment.

Limits

  • Authentication and atomic external storage are assumptions, not results.
  • Real acceptance, deployment and rollback of a contract registry were not run; mass revision of a whole base and an external server current head were not tested.
  • Results of the 36 planned setups are absent, as are 9 sequential reviews. Four hypotheses (separated states, typed dependencies, partial-publication reconciliation, versioned canon) are untested.
  • Two numbers from different instruments (786/12 and 772/0) are not comparable; the qualification report MM_A10474 was not read.

Status and what is open

  • The late research synthesis gate is open.
  • Independent semantic review of this text has not been performed.
  • The English page is a summary, not a translation, and does not replace the Russian original.
  • Not integrated and still missing: the NAV reports, the TIM reports, MM_OBLIGATIONS_20261030 together with the previous-stage report, the runs behind section 7 of the knowledge-admission amendment (MM_KA0004), and the items ING-01 to ING-08.
  • The crosswalk content is a one-assistant self-check, not independent confirmation.
