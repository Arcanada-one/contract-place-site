Knowledge Contract is an open scientific and engineering programme for representing meaning and using knowledge under explicit conditions. The programme connects a question, an interpreted source, a formal representation, a justified operation, an implementation and an observed result. It distinguishes what a source says, what has been established, what applies in a context, and what an agent is authorized to do.

What is available, and what is being developed

The proposed edition-1.1 catalogue in PR89 contains 31 works: ten scientific manuscripts (including the research-draft Meaning Bridge and newly authored, independently scoped-reviewed research-draft Meaning Geometry), eleven specifications, six studies and four reference/appendix documents. The accepted earlier catalogue retains 30 works until a separate integration decision. The author-approved target structure contains 34 works: ten scientific works, thirteen specifications, seven studies and four reference/appendix documents. Three additional engineering works remain planned, not completed or released; two other foundations develop existing manuscripts without duplicate papers.

This guide implements the author’s decision to make the foundations and their connections explicit. The PR89 catalogue proposal adds one identified research-draft source without changing its reviewed mathematical bytes, earlier research results, prior accepted source versions, corpus admission or an execution task. Full manuscripts and their actual rendition/review states remain authoritative. Updating a heading alone cannot complete a research programme.

The three titles name the programme’s foundations. They do not claim that one version captures all human understanding. Theory supplies the objects and questions; algebra studies justified operations; geometry studies distinguishability and comparison. Neither mathematical perspective automatically proves the other.

Use explicit relation labels: uses-definition, proves-under, operationalizes, tests, limits, and supersedes. A reading link is not a proof dependency. A counterexample must identify its exact claim and premises. An implementation failure, failed empirical hypothesis and refuted theorem need different repairs. Feedback from checks may revise the relevant scientific or engineering claim without erasing the earlier evidence.

The current roadmap remains the research plan, and its existing execution authority remains unchanged. This reading map does not create a second task tracker or declare any existing task complete. The public names Knowledge Checks and Execution Checks replace opaque case-family labels in reader prose; stable case identifiers remain in technical records.

Publication and contribution

Release already accepted finite reading batches without waiting for every planned foundation or experiment. New full manuscripts enter the catalogue only when they exist and carry their actual status. Keep old slugs, citations, anchors and exact review histories; a substantive change needs a new version and scoped review, not a cosmetic repinning of old approval. Update affected translations against exact source changes rather than restarting unchanged translations.

The author is Pavel Valentov. This programme and its localized guides are original editorial work assisted by KC-Brain, not independent scientific review. Text is CC BY 4.0. Contributions may propose corrections, reproducible examples, counterarguments and implementations through the contribution process. State the exact work/revision, evidence and limits. A contribution from an agent is assessed by its content and traceability, not counted as independent evidence merely because another agent repeats it.

Publishing this guide establishes the public structure and reading map. It does not establish completion of the three remaining planned engineering works, an unrestricted new scientific theorem, deployment, server indexing or permission for an agent to act. Meaning Geometry remains a bounded research-draft proposal until ordinary source and publication acceptance.
