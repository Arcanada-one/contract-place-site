A public home for Knowledge Contract

Knowledge should carry its conditions.

Knowledge Contract is a research effort toward a verifiable way to decide what an agent may know, reuse, and act on. Its current target is 3.0.0-draft.1; the specification and experiments remain in progress. Contract Place will host the rules, papers, and implementation resources as they become ready.
