Knowledge Contract Arcana

We study how people and agents can reuse knowledge while keeping its source, scope, evidence, and conditions of use visible. The Knowledge Contract target is 3.0.0-draft.1; the specification and experiments remain in progress. This site will host reviewed rules, papers, and implementation resources as they become ready.
