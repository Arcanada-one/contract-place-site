Knowledge Contract Arcana

A research programme for the lifecycle of knowledge.

Knowledge Contract Arcana studies a unified contract for knowledge reuse: from sources and representations to bounded context, evidence, revisions and conditions of application. The published research is open; the processing engine remains proprietary.
